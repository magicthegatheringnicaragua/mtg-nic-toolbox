# Background Gallery Attribution

This directory holds selectable full-background images for the "Fondo"
gallery picker (see `src/backgrounds.mjs`). The default "Rayas Azules"
background is not an image file - it is the procedurally-drawn striped
background in `src/render.mjs` (`drawStripedBackground()`).

## echoverse-reveal.jpg

Reality Fracture reveal/key art via TCGplayer's article "Reality Fracture
Twists All of Your Favorite Cards and Creatures...". It depicts a hooded,
dark-blue figure's face with glowing eyes beside an explosive golden
fractured-city vista. The exact card and artist have not been
independently confirmed, so this is credited generically as Reality
Fracture reveal/key art rather than to a specific named card or artist.
